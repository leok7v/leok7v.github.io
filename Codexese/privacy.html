Codexese Privacy Policy

November 21, 2026

Short version: Codexese collects nothing. No account, no server, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

What we collect

Nothing. Codexese has no backend and no database of users. It does not send what you write, or the pictures you open, anywhere. There are no analytics, no crash reporters and no advertising identifiers in the app.

What stays on your device

The text you write, the pages Codexese makes from it, and the pictures you open to read are processed on your iPhone, iPad or Mac. Pages are kept only in temporary files of the app until you save or share them. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Network

Codexese does not use the network. It works the same in airplane mode. When you share a page, it goes where you choose to send it, through the share sheet of your device, and that app or service has its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Photos: when you choose Save Image in the share sheet, your device asks to let Codexese add the picture to Photos. Codexese only adds it. To open a picture, you pick it yourself, and Codexese gets only that picture.
  • Share extension: when you share a picture from Photos to Codexese, the extension puts the picture in a folder on your device that only Codexese can read, and opens the app. Nothing leaves the device.

The QR code on the page

Every page has a small round QR code in the corner. It holds only the address of the Codexese web page. It does not hold your text, and it does not track who scans it.

Children's privacy

Codexese collects no personal information from anybody, children included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
