On-device inference stacks and the apps built on them. Each links to its own site.

im.ai
---

Private on-device LLM chat for iPhone, iPad, and Mac — run models locally on `llama.cpp`, or connect to cloud providers when you want to.

<a href="https://leok7v.github.io/im.ai/" target="_blank">leok7v.github.io/im.ai</a>

Talking Frog
---

A friendly voice companion for macOS and iOS that listens and answers out loud — on-device, private, no account.

<a href="https://leok7v.github.io/talking.frog/" target="_blank">leok7v.github.io/talking.frog</a>

Inference Stacks
---

Anatomy of eight LLM inference engines — how on-device and edge inference stacks are actually put together, side by side.

<a href="https://leok7v.github.io/inference-stacks/" target="_blank">leok7v.github.io/inference-stacks</a>

md.too
---

A minimalist, read-only Markdown viewer for macOS and iOS. Native Swift, zero third-party dependencies.

<a href="https://leok7v.github.io/md.too/" target="_blank">leok7v.github.io/md.too</a>

ChatOKF
---

On-device chat for macOS and iOS — the whole model runs on your own hardware, in pure Swift on Metal. What it remembers is plain text in the Open Knowledge Format, so it outlives the model that wrote it. No C, no Python, no FFI.

<a href="https://leok7v.github.io/ChatOKF/" target="_blank">leok7v.github.io/ChatOKF</a>
